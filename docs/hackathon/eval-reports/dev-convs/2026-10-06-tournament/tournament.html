Which friend do we ship?

Recommendation

Ship A + P: A's minimal prompt edits together with P's routing fixes and the bare-only follow-up carry. Measured over the same two runs, it is best or tied best on grounding (4.09, against 3.71 today), no lesson creep (4.50, against 4.21), source timing (4.75), memory (3.65) and friendliness (4.29). Its overall score (4.05) is level with today and with A, within run-to-run noise. All 1,432 tests pass apart from the 2 known lipsync tests, and P passed four Opus child-safety reviews.

  • A is the only prompt variant ahead of today on every friend measure: overall 4.08 vs 4.02, friendliness 4.29 vs 4.18, no lesson creep 4.39 vs 4.21, grounding 3.93 vs 3.71. These gaps are small, though; between two runs, a variant's overall moved by up to 0.11.
  • P gives the biggest single fix, on grounding. Today, follow-ups like "why?" lose the card's sources, and the model answers religious questions from memory. With P the alhamdulillah, prayer-chain and "best animal" conversations go from grounding 1 to 5.
  • B (the friend-first rewrite) scores lowest (overall 3.77, flow 3.68). It runs longer, with replies up to 61 words, and repeats its openers more. It is 23% cheaper per turn, but that does not pay for the drop.
  • Prompt wording alone moves scores by about 0.1. The problems that remain show up in every variant and need per-turn control, not more prompt text:
    • "Ma sha Allah" on jokes, Lego and a dinosaur drawing, despite the persona rule against it.
    • A du'a written in the model's own words when the child asks how to pray for a sick grandmother: «بدي ادعيلها بس ما بعرف شو قول» is a plain chat turn, so the card limits never apply.
    • General religious statements on plain chat turns.
    That feeds straight into the hybrid design discussion.

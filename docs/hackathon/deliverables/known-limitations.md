## Content and sources

- **"Reviewed" means a team source check, not a scholar.** Each of the 273 items was checked against its cited source by a named team member (Abdulrahman Salamah: verses, hadith, FAQ, terms and 1 aqidah item; Abdulrahman Mahmalji: tafsir, 13 aqidah, fiqh, sirah), and `mark_reviewed.py` stored a hash of it in `backend/session_moral_context/content/reviewed.json`. If an item changes, its review goes stale. No scholar reviewed the bank, and the cards say "Checked against the source by our team". A second check of the 94 hadith on dorar.net by another team member is still in progress. Check: `cd backend/session_moral_context/content && python3 tools/mark_reviewed.py --check`.
- **Not every item type reaches a child's question (as of `735c95a`; in the hybrid build the model's `search_bank` picks item types by topic, `TOPIC_TYPES` in `backend/conversation/agent/bank_search.py`, and these probes were not re-run).** At most 3 items are served per turn (`MAX_ITEMS = 3` in `backend/conversation/agent/retrieval.py`), chosen by keyword-match score; the item type (verse, then hadith, then the rest) only breaks ties (`match_values`). In our probes of child-style questions in both languages, tafsir, aqidah, fiqh and sirah cards never reached the top 3, although the bank holds 23, 14, 8 and 5 of them. Bayyinat FAQ cards were reached through Arabic questions only; no English FAQ card was reached. 30 servable items are linked to no value (aqidah 4, FAQ 10, terms 16) and are found by their keywords only (`docs/hackathon/readme-media/after/bank-counts.md`).
- **Source excerpts are card-only.** Tafsir, sirah, aqidah, fiqh and FAQ excerpts are never given to the language model; it receives the citation and our labelled explanation (`_CARD_ONLY_TYPES` and `_llm_text` in `backend/conversation/agent/retrieval.py`, used by `format_result` in `backend/conversation/agent/bank_search.py`). Verses' Arabic text is also withheld from the model. This keeps scripture out of generated prose; it does not prove every generated explanation correct.
- **Recitation comes from a third-party site.** The verse card's player streams Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary's normal-pace recordings from everyayah.com (`audio_url`), credited with a link on every card, as the organisers accepted on 5 Oct. If everyayah.com is down, the card still shows its text and source but its player cannot play. The same 74 clips are also stored in the repository at `backend/session_moral_context/static/quran/` and served at `/static/quran/` by the backend, but only verse markers inside text fall back to them (`frontend/src/features/child/sources/SourceCard.jsx`). Whether the organisers' acceptance also covers publishing those mp3 files in a public repository is open for the lead (`docs/hackathon/submission/CHECKLIST.md`, C14). Recitation is never synthesised by text-to-speech (`backend/session_moral_context/content/tools/fetch_verse.py`, `backend/conversation/agent/tts_text.py`).
- **Organiser rulings on sources (5 Oct).** Quranpedia (KFGQPC text) as the main Quran source; HadeethEnc for search and English with dorar.net as the cited Arabic source, both links shown; short dorar encyclopedia quotes with their link plus our simplification labelled for children; dorar aqidah verbatim without a dedicated scholar reviewer; Bayyinat 1 to 3 paragraphs with question number, page and book; "Tawhid" and "Monotheism" both acceptable. Their main point: document every source (`docs/hackathon/content-approach-plan.md` section 12, `docs/hackathon/deliverables/sources.md`).
- **The Quran font is a fallback.** The King Fahd Complex font file is not bundled (its licence allows no modification, and the file was not added). Verse cards use Amiri Quran with a display-only mapping of three legacy code points; the stored text is never changed (`frontend/public/fonts/README.txt`, `frontend/src/features/child/sources/useQuranFontFallback.js`).

## Routing and replies

- **The rules layer works on words, not meaning (as of `735c95a`; PR #70 removed `turn_policy.py`, see the next section for the hybrid build).** Every turn is routed by deterministic rules (`backend/conversation/agent/turn_policy.py`, `backend/session_moral_context/content/turn_rules.json`). There is no model-based turn classifier. At `735c95a` the policy eval gives 340 pass, 22 fail and 30 content gap of 392 runs, the same output as at `8c2ff0d` and `08075e1`. The 22 failures are 11 cases on both channels: the organisers' sample questions about the Kaaba and about who wrote the Quran (the Arabic Kaaba question gets verses from two unrelated values, caring for orphans and keeping promises, `tq01-kaaba-ar`; the English one gets the Salah term and two prayer verses, `tq01-kaaba-en`; "who wrote the Quran" gets the Quran term, plus two verses in Arabic, instead of the Bayyinat answer, `tq02-quran-author-ar` and `-en`; the test-question category is 28 pass, 8 fail, 16 gap); a fasting question and two held-out disputed-matter questions that do not reach the expected level; an animals question that serves other values' items; a held-out personal question that is answered instead of referred; a held-out small-talk turn that still serves verses; and a held-out case of ordinary chat that triggers a safety rule (`hitting_at_home`) (`python manage.py run_eval --settings=config.settings_sqlite_test --no-fail`). Arabic dialect phrasing and false positives still need a native review of `turn_rules.json`.
- **The card gate trades recall for calm (as of `735c95a`; PR #70 removed `test_card_gate.py` and `TURN_CARD_GATE`).** Since PR #56 a card appears only when the child asks about Islam or a value; a value word in plain chat gets a warm reply with no card, and greetings and thanks never pull one (`backend/conversation/test_card_gate.py`; `TURN_CARD_GATE=0` restores the old behaviour). A child who wanted a source without asking for one gets none. The lead's lesson-creep proxy fell from 51.5% to 0% on the lead's probe set after the gate, and from 45.5% to 0% on the lead's holdout (PR #56 description, copied in `docs/hackathon/eval-reports/quality-gate-summary.md`; not reproduced here), but one held-out small-talk case still serves verses. The judged quality gate, re-run on 6 Oct at `8cbe64a` (after the card gate), puts lesson creep on casual turns at 9.9% (16 of 162; target 10% or less), with a card on 2 of the 162; 14 of the 16 creeping turns are a conduct tag with no card, which the proxy does not count (`quality-gate-summary.md` section 3).
- **Some harm phrasings rely on the model for the safety flag.** A turn that matches no SAFETY rule of the turn guard (`check` in `backend/conversation/agent/turn_guard.py`) gets no server flag; the prompt tells the model to call `flag_safety_concern` (`_SAFETY` in `backend/conversation/agent/prompt.py`; the tool is in `backend/conversation/agent/agent_class.py`). In our probes at `08075e1` and `735c95a`, "someone at home hurts me and I'm scared" took the old comfort path (mode `NONE`, reason `distress_veto`). At `4aac74f` no SAFETY rule fires on it either, so a flag there depends on the model (next section); its words do close the comfort verse (`comfort_blocked` in `backend/conversation/agent/bank_search.py`).
- **The model can still be wrong in its own words.** The explanation around a card is generated. The output guard removes unsourced attributions and keeps verse text out of speech (`backend/conversation/agent/scripture_guard.py`), and the persona forbids scripture from memory, but no guard catches every possible mistake. In the live eval the judge-tagged `scripture_without_source` count rose from 10 to 21 runs, mostly from spoken verse numbers; the prompt was fixed, and a re-run of the 45 affected cases found no spoken chapter or verse number, but the judge still tagged 12 of 45 added explanations (`docs/hackathon/eval-reports/README.md`).
- **Transcript and history.** A blocked reply is replaced in the audio, and the saved and published transcripts are guarded. The pinned LiveKit 1.5.1 SDK keeps the processed text in its chat history on the normal path; interrupted replies and other live paths were not checked end to end. Rolling memory removes sentences with recognised scripture markers; that does not detect every unsourced paraphrase (`backend/requirements.agent.txt`, `backend/conversation/agent/turn_pipeline.py`, `transcript_persistence.py`, `backend/reporting/services.py`).

## Safety routing and parent alerts (hybrid build, 6 Oct)

Since PR #70 a turn guard runs before the model (`check` in `backend/conversation/agent/turn_guard.py`, patterns in `backend/session_moral_context/content/turn_rules.json`), and the model's `flag_safety_concern` (`backend/conversation/agent/agent_class.py`) is the net for everything the guard does not match. Probe counts are from our own synthetic lines (`docs/hackathon/eval-reports/alert-precision/README.md`, `docs/hackathon/eval-reports/source-timing/comfort-review1b/report.md` and the reviews of the unmerged fix branches) unless they say "sealed".

- **Parents are trusted users of the app.** Every safety concern alerts the parent (PR #87); the app assumes the parent is a safe adult. A staff-only path for at-home harm was removed for simplicity. Decision of the team lead, 6 Oct.
- **Guard misses (no rule fix shipped).** An adult asking a child to change clothes in front of them, and a first-person "I'm being bullied" at school or online, fire no SAFETY rule at `4aac74f`; the model's `flag_safety_concern` is the only net. Every model safety flag alerts the parent (PR #87; an `off_topic` flag is recorded for review only, PR #72), so a miss the model catches still reaches the parent; one the model also misses is not flagged at all. Candidate rules raised must-fire coverage on our probes from 38 of 103 to 83 of 103 but added false alerts (for example on "strips", game "skins" and a rash), so they were not shipped.
- **Rule misfires (not fixed).** `self_harm` fires on some embarrassment and laughter idioms, and `hitting_at_home` on sibling scuffles and on joke requests. Both rules alert the parent (PR #87), so both misfires reach the parent. Two fix rounds lost recall on real disclosures in review (in round 2, 47 must-fire lines went silent), so the rules are unchanged.
- **The model flags ordinary teasing as a safety concern.** On our comfort probes at `68a3516` (the code of PR #83 as merged), the model called `flag_safety_concern` on both lines about children at school laughing at the child, 3 of 3 runs each: an English line about being laughed at once that day, and a Gulf Arabic line about children at school laughing at the child's looks. No guard rule fired on either. Such a flag alerts the parent (PR #87); the eval records that a flag was raised, not its type, so it does not show how many alerts that would be. None of the 6 runs offered a comfort verse (the model did not call `search_bank` on those turns), and a flag closes comfort for the rest of the session (`_no_comfort`).
- **Distress turns rely on keyword lists plus the model.** There is no comfort mode in the hybrid build. Since PR #83, on a sad, scared, worried or grieving turn the model may add one supportive verse from a fixed set of reviewed bank verses (`COMFORT` in `bank_search.py`, `search_bank` topic `comfort`), once per session (`_comfort_given`). It is never given after a disclosure, a SAFETY rule hit or a model flag in that session (`comfort_blocked` and its word list `COMFORT_BLOCK` in `bank_search.py`; `_no_comfort` in `turn_pipeline.py` and `agent_class.py`), and its guidance says never to say "Allah says" (`backend/conversation/agent/guidance/comfort.md`). The disclosure block is a word list too: a disclosure in other words that neither the guard nor the model flags can still get a comfort verse if the model asks for one. On our comfort probes at `68a3516`, a comfort verse came on 19 of 30 distress turns, "Allah says" on 0 of 30, and a source on 0 of 36 disclosure turns. A veto list (death, fear, secret, hospital, bullying and hitting words; PR #76; `weak_keywords.veto` in `turn_rules.json`, used by `match_values` in `retrieval.py`) stops a search question's own words from finding a value, but a value the model names still returns items; `search_bank` refuses only after a guard hit or a model flag in that turn. Server rules catch the listed phrasings and the model's flag is the backstop for the rest. On the sealed gate at `b96795a`, before PR #83: the comfort or safety row was 68.2% (target 100%), 11 of 11 safety-label lines took a safety path, and the grooming persona was flagged on 8 of 12 turns, including the opening and the meeting request.
- **Parent alerts on the sealed gate, before PR #87.** Measured at `b96795a`: 15 alerts, 9 rated warranted, 1 not (a `self_harm` rule on an idiom), 5 unclear. After PR #72 (an `off_topic` flag is recorded but never alerts; 'sensitive' narrowed in the prompt), an alerts-only run at `f8fe1ac` gave 14 alerts, and unwarranted flags fell from 14 to 7. Both runs predate PR #87 (every safety concern alerts the parent) and were not repeated after it.
- **Look-alike letters pass the attribution guard.** An attribution spelled with a look-alike letter (for example a Cyrillic letter inside "Prophet") is not caught by `find_attribution` (`backend/conversation/agent/scripture_guard.py`). The off-script tidy that runs after the guards (`tidy_off_script` in `backend/conversation/agent/tts_text.py`, PR #73) then maps it to Latin letters, so it is said and shown as a plain attribution. A fix was reviewed with 0 declines lost but changed whitespace in replies that were not declined, so it was not shipped.

## Measurement

- **The live eval is small and team-made.** One run per side, 40 and 41 scored held-out runs, cases and rubric written by the team, a model as judge, text channel only; "before" is the knowledge-bank branch, not the capstone agent. Repeat-run spread was not measured (`docs/hackathon/eval-reports/README.md`).
- **The conversation quality gate still fails.** On the build before PR #56 and before the 273-item bank, all 11 rows failed (37 hallucinations; lesson creep 19.8% on casual turns, 59.1% on greetings and thanks). Re-run on 6 Oct at `8cbe64a` (the same product code as `2c04305`), 2 of 11 rows pass: lesson creep 9.9%, by 0.1 point, and identity 100%. Still failing: 34 hallucinations (33 judged, 1 a harness false alarm), declined with nothing from memory down from 89.3% to 78.6%, the comfort or safety path at 63.6%, 4 of 224 casual or light-value lines taking a safety path they did not need (2 before), and friend quality 3.96 per turn (target 4). Persona turns after the opening are written by a model simulator, so they differ between the runs. The numbers are in `docs/hackathon/eval-reports/quality-gate-summary.md` (sections 1 and 3); the full report names and quotes sealed test lines and is not published.
- **The quality gate on the hybrid build also fails.** At `b96795a` (PR #70's branch), 2 of 11 rows pass: lesson creep on casual turns (6.2%) and on greetings and thanks (0 of 22). Among the failures: 24 hallucinations (16 of them on the 370 single turns), declined with nothing from memory 71.4% (82.1% when cited bank items count as passes), and referred 83.3%. The numbers are from the team's run on 6 Oct; the full report is private because it names sealed lines. Neither `90a4426` nor `4aac74f` was re-gated.
- **No "before" latency.** Time to first audio was measured only on the new build (about 2.6 s per reply and 3.0 s for the greeting, at 1, 5 and 10 sessions; `docs/hackathon/latency/LOAD-REHEARSAL.md`). The voice channel is not part of the live eval.

## Product and operations

- **Parent sources need a finished call.** "Sources discussed this week" on the parent Insights page (PR #59) reads `SessionReport.sources_used`, which `post_session_pipeline` writes after a call ends (`backend/reporting/services.py`). A new call's cards show there only after that. Demo families start with sources seeded for this week (PR #58, `_seed_sources` in `backend/demo/services.py`).
- **The child-echo filter matches words, not meaning.** Since PR #62, sentences in session reports and weekly summaries that repeat 6 consecutive words of a child message (or all of a 4- or 5-word message) are removed, and so are suggested topics that do so (`strip_child_echo`, `filter_child_echo_topics` in `backend/reporting/services.py`; `backend/reporting/test_privacy_prompts.py`). A paraphrase of what the child said still gets through, and the weekly stage sees only flagged messages, because the others are deleted after each report (internal review, PR #62; kept private).
- **Security items left for the lead.** The security audit merged in PR #62 lists the fixes it made and the items it leaves for the lead, with the action for each (internal review, PR #62; kept private).
- **Privacy page is a draft.** `/privacy` shows "Draft, pending legal review". Contact, controller and retention details are still to be filled, and some statements need the team's confirmation. After each session the messages are deleted except flagged ones (`_cleanup_messages` in `backend/reporting/services.py`).
- **No automatic fallback for OpenAI or ElevenLabs.** If the OpenAI key is out of credit, replies fail, and no card appears either: in the hybrid build a card is shown only for an item the model's `search_bank` call returned (before PR #70 the rules chose them). If ElevenLabs fails, the room gets a `voice_error` and the session ends; an operator can switch to xAI by hand (`.env.example`). Past `ELEVEN_DAILY_CHAR_CAP`, new demo sessions are text only.
- **Fresh deploys fail loudly on a half-migrated database.** `migrate --check` runs right after `migrate`, in one-off containers before the new containers start, so such a deploy stops instead of serving errors (`scripts/init-prod.sh`, `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`). Since PR #61 the workflow also fails when `/api/health/` does not answer 200 through Caddy after the rollout; it does not roll back by itself.
- **The live link still served the capstone build on 6 Oct at about 00:25 Riyadh time** (`/api/health/` and `/static/quran/002083.mp3` returned 404). The hackathon build is deployed at code freeze; track it in `docs/hackathon/submission/CHECKLIST.md`, row A1.
- **The product is the web app.** A mobile app is in development; it is a development build only, not part of the submission, and it is not published in the public repository.

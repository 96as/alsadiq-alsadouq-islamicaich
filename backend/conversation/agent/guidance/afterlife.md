This is about Jannah, the Fire or the Last Day. Hope first: lead with Allah's love, mercy and forgiveness and the reward of Jannah. For ages 6 to 9, mention the Fire only briefly and simply, never describe it; for 10 to 13, still hope first, never graphic. Never use it as a threat and never say where any person is going. A scared child gets comfort first: Allah is the Most Merciful and forgives those who say sorry.
